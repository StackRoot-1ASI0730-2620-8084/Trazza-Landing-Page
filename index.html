How does Trazza work in
Metropolitan Lima?

Direct-link technology that syncs carriers' regular routes with one-off shipments from businesses and workshops.

1

Post your route and availability

Register your initial drop-off point (e.g. a warehouse in Lurín or Callao) and your final return point. Specify your vehicle's free tonnage and volume in cubic meters.

Takes less than 45 seconds from your phone
2

Get AI-powered cargo alerts

Our algorithm scans businesses along your route that need to move packages or goods, and sends you a notification with the guaranteed net rate.

No more than 1.8 km off your main route
3

Accept, haul and get paid

Make the pickup, follow the assisted map with real-time telemetry, and receive a secure, direct payment to your bank account or digital wallet when you deliver the cargo.

Instant settlement with no extra fees
